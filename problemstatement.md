# Problem Statement: AI-Based Navigation System

## 1. Real-World Problem
Navigating inside large, complex buildings—such as malls, airports, hospitals, and corporate campuses—is a common challenge. 
*   **GPS Limitations:** Standard GPS is often inaccurate or completely non-functional indoors.
*   **Static Maps:** Physical "You Are Here" maps are static, often outdated, and difficult to interpret for precise navigation.
*   **Information Overload:** Users often struggle to find specific rooms, departments, or facilities in sprawling environments.

## 2. Solution: AI-Based Navigation System
This project provides a digital, interactive indoor navigation solution that leverages AI to transform static blueprints into dynamic navigation maps.

### Core Features & Solutions:
*   **AI-Powered Blueprint Analysis:** Automatically processes uploaded building blueprints (images) to identify key locations (nodes) and traversable paths (edges), eliminating manual mapping efforts.
*   **Google Maps Integration:** Links indoor maps with their real-world geographic coordinates for a seamless transition from outdoor to indoor navigation.
*   **Interactive Pathfinding:** Provides users with visual, step-by-step routing from their current location (e.g., a QR code entry point) to their desired destination.
*   **QR-Based Accessibility:** Buildings can be accessed instantly by scanning a generated QR code, which directs users to the building's digital map.
*   **Administrative Dashboard:** A central hub for building managers to upload blueprints, manage building information, and view navigation statistics.

## 3. Goals
*   Simplify indoor navigation for end-users.
*   Automate the creation of indoor maps using AI image processing.
*   Provide a scalable and user-friendly platform for building management.
